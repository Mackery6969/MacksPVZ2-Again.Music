## Where the files came from

The audio was extracted from the **PvZ2 Gardendless** (PVZGE) web build, itself
an unofficial fan reimplementation using *Plants vs. Zombies 2* assets. Clip
names are the originals recovered from that build's asset packs.

PVZGE's own User Agreement is explicit about the split, and it governs here:

> All art resources, music, sound effects, character images (plants, zombies,
> etc.), and related design elements contained in this Project belong to the
> original copyright holders, Electronic Arts / PopCap Games, and other
> relevant rights holders.

> The Web build version content of this game is open-sourced under the GNU
> General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0).

So **GPL-3.0 covers PVZGE's code, not these assets.** PVZGE holds no rights to
the *Plants vs. Zombies 2* audio and grants none. Nothing in this repository is
GPL-licensed by way of PVZGE.

The same agreement also states:

> Unauthorized separate distribution or sale of proprietary assets within the
> game is strictly prohibited.

That clause is a community norm, not a grant of rights. PVZGE distributes the
same PopCap audio in every build, and holds no authority to permit or prohibit
anything about it - nobody can forbid redistribution of material they do not
own. The distinction it reaches for, between assets embedded in a playable fan
work and assets published on their own, carries no legal weight.

The constraint that does apply, to PVZGE and to this repository alike, is
Electronic Arts' and PopCap's copyright. Neither project is licensed; both
depend on the rights holders' forbearance. A standalone pile of extracted audio
is simply the most conspicuous shape that can take, which is why this
repository is treated as a working copy rather than a distribution channel.
